G2 Coach Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 5, 2026

G2 Coach is a free practice app for Ontario G2 road-test maneuvers, made by Jun Zhao (junzhaodesign.com). This policy explains what the app does and doesn't do with your information.

What we collect

Nothing that identifies you. G2 Coach has no accounts, no sign-in, no ads and no analytics or tracking tools.

What stays on your phone

The app remembers which maneuvers you have finished so it can show your progress. This is saved only on your device, in the app's local storage. It is never sent to us or anyone else. Clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app deletes it.

Services the app connects to

  • junzhaodesign.com hosts the app. Like most websites, the host may keep standard server logs (such as IP address, device type and time of request) for security and to keep the site running.
  • Google Fonts supplies the handwritten typefaces. Your device requests them from Google, which receives your IP address as part of that request. See Google's privacy policy.

Children

G2 Coach is meant for learner drivers aged 16 and over. It is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.

Not an official app

G2 Coach is an unofficial practice tool. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or connected to the Ontario Ministry of Transportation (MTO), DriveTest or any government body. Always check the official Ontario Driver's Handbook.
